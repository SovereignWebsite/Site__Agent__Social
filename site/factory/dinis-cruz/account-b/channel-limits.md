# Channel limits and media rules, first pass (2026-11-28T02:38Z)

Status: FIRST PASS from web search summaries on 28 Nov 2026. I have not yet opened each primary page. Treat every figure as "to confirm" before it goes into a post card. Where sources disagree I say so and give no single number.

## YouTube (Shorts)
- Title up to 100 characters; description up to 5,000: YouTube Help, "Edit video settings" https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404 (regular videos; third-party guides say Shorts follow the same, no Shorts-specific page found).
- Shorts length: up to 3 minutes since 15 Oct 2024 (third-party sources: Descript, CapCut); confirm on YouTube Help.
- Titles are cut off in some views (third-party guesses range 40 to 70 characters): put the point first.
- Buffer note: its article says YouTube's API is stricter than the native uploader.

## Instagram (Reels)
- Caption up to 2,200 characters; preview cuts near 125 (third-party: Sendible). Reels length sources conflict (90 s, 3 min, 20 min): CONFIRM on Instagram's own help.
- Hashtags: sources conflict (30 vs a claimed cut to 5 in Dec 2025): CONFIRM before using more than a few.
- Buffer note: Reels file under 100 MB (Buffer, "Sharing videos through Buffer" https://support.buffer.com/article/616-uploading-and-sharing-links-to-videos).

## LinkedIn (organic video)
- File 75 KB to 5 GB (LinkedIn Help, answer 85306 is the ads page; organic page to confirm: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7486279).
- Length sources conflict (10 vs 15 minutes): CONFIRM. Post text limit not found: CONFIRM (ad intro text guidance is 150 characters recommended, 600 maximum is not stated here).
- Captions as SRT for ads; thumbnails 2 MB JPG or PNG (ads).

## Buffer (media)
- Source: https://support.buffer.com/article/935-troubleshooting-video-uploads-in-buffer and https://support.buffer.com/article/616-uploading-and-sharing-links-to-videos
- Upload from device or add media by link; the same rules apply to both.
- Codec matters more than size: avoid ProRes, HEVC (H.265), AV1, RED; use H.264 MP4.
- Length and aspect ratio limits come from each network's API.
- You cannot upload a separate cover image for a video in Buffer; you can only pick a frame from the video. So the cover frame must be IN the video (first 600 ms or a chosen frame).
- Not found: scheduling rules for these formats, and whether a link must be public; CONFIRM.

## Spotify for Creators (podcast audio), for suno.b
- Formats: MP3, M4A, WAV; mono or stereo. No hard file-size cap stated; larger files process slower and fail more on weak connections. Keep embedded artwork and ID3 metadata small. Episode artwork is added under "Additional details" at publishing. Source: https://support.spotify.com/uk/creators/article/publishing-audio-episodes/
- Not found: cover-art pixel size and limits; CONFIRM on Spotify's podcast artwork page.
- Spotify's terms bar uploading audio that belongs to someone else: so Suno licence and the ElevenLabs terms need a clear answer first.
