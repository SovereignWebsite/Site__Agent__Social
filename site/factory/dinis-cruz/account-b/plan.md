# b.social plan v0.1 (26 Nov 2026)

## Weekly flow
1. Mon: I read the week's material (studio.b, b.suno, site keepers) and write a one-page content calendar: one row per post, per channel (Instagram, LinkedIn, others), with date, slot, source, owner.
2. Tue-Wed: agents hand me finished material. instagram.b shapes Instagram versions; I write LinkedIn and other versions. Each post is a small JSON card: channel, text, media paths, alt text, labels, scheduled time, source link, checks.
3. Thu: my checks against the card: b.security's presenter conditions (synthetic label inside the image, caption says "synthetic presenter" and "voice made by AI", stock voices, character names only, EXIF stripped), no private details, no invented facts, British English, no em dashes. Failures go back to the maker.
4. Thu: one approval pack for Dinis (a single page: each post as it will appear, with its time).
5. Dinis approves, edits or rejects each post. Only approved cards move on.
6. A person puts approved posts into Buffer (Buffer schedules and releases). I never post, schedule or use a connector.
7. Fri: I note what went out and what to learn, and plan the next week.

## What each agent hands me
- studio.b: final images, video, covers, with the synthetic label burned in, a caption draft and alt text, source and licence/credit line.
- b.suno: song or episode audio, title, presenter script, liked-song list, credit line; "voice made by AI" flagged.
- site keepers: the page URL, a 2-line summary, one image, what is new and why it matters.
- instagram.b: Instagram-ready version (crop, hashtags, reel cover) and its specialist advice; it keeps the Instagram platform site.
- b.security: rulings; b.outreach: any post touching Buffer; I do not publish that before they approve.

## Approval for Dinis
One pack per week, each post marked: approve / change (with note) / drop. Nothing is scheduled without a recorded approve. Presenter 'realist' style and any news-style frame need his explicit yes. Approval is per post and per version, not blanket.

## Platform sites (b.sites builds)
Buffer: the formula in plain steps: Claude with the studio makes the materials; several agents prepare them; the social media agent coordinates and checks; a person approves; Buffer schedules and releases. Written for people who say "I don't know LinkedIn, I need to do my posts". Honest comparison of scheduling services (only sourced, dated facts; no prices I cannot cite). A worked week from Dinis's own flow. Unofficial, not made or endorsed by Buffer.
LinkedIn: the same formula for LinkedIn: post types, what the agents prepare, what LinkedIn's own rules and API allow (sourced, dated), AI-content labelling, how a person approves. Unofficial, not endorsed by LinkedIn. Keep the Instagram site as the model and cross-link the three.

## Open points for Dinis
Which channels beyond Instagram and LinkedIn; posting frequency; approval day and time; who clicks into Buffer.
